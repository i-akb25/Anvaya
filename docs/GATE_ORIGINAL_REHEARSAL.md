# Engineering depth and GATE original rehearsal · 18 October 2026

## Delivered

GATE EE, EC and IN classrooms now offer full-count original rehearsals under their Practice section. Each paper has 65 distinct questions and 100 available marks: GA 15, engineering mathematics 13 and technical foundations 72. Standard practice has one 180-minute whole-paper timer; compensatory practice has 240 minutes. This profile is a study setting, not accommodation eligibility approval.

The primary rule reference is the [official GATE2027 question-paper pattern](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/question_paper_pattern), rechecked 18 October 2026. Wrong one-mark MCQs lose 1/3; wrong two-mark MCQs lose 2/3. Wrong MSQ/NAT and blank responses score 0. MSQ requires an exact answer set with no partial credit. Whole-paper question navigation remains available until submission/deadline.

These are original foundation questions, not official PYQs or calibrated mocks. The 9-mathematics/46-technical question allocation, the 1/2-mark assignment within each domain and topic sampling are rehearsal choices. The official reference specifies the marks split and overall 65-question count, not this exact subtopic or format distribution. Full communications, machines, power, instrumentation and other advanced subject breadth are not represented by these foundation pools.

The immutable template uses bank v7 and `gate-original-2027-v1`. GA has 5 one-mark and 5 two-mark questions; mathematics has 5 one-mark and 4 two-mark questions; technical has 20 one-mark and 26 two-mark questions. Thus there are 30 one-mark and 35 two-mark questions overall. Validators reject changes to marks, question domains, counts, paper identity, navigation, deadlines or bank version. Mixed SSC/GATE metadata is rejected. Bankv1–v6 remain available unchanged, and existing full SSC templates stay pinned to bank v5.

The GA pool currently permits one unseen full attempt per exam context. Exhaustion stops generation rather than padding or silently reusing a question. Later attempts require an explicit reuse choice. Every individual paper still contains unique question IDs. A reshuffle is not a newly calibrated paper. Attempts use the existing matching exam/ID/writer launch token, active-session blocks, frozen answers, revision conflicts, retained deadline, explicit takeover, paged palettes/review and validated backups. Reports show 15/13/72 practice components without a normalised score, rank or qualifying verdict.

## Scientific calculator

`/tools/calculator` provides a local real-number scientific practice calculator, also available inside GATE attempts. It is not a replica of the official calculator. It supports explicit arithmetic, right-associative powers, factorial, percentages, parentheses, scientific notation, pi/e/previous answer, square root, absolute value, natural/base 10 logs, exponential and trigonometric/inverse functions with explicit degree/radian mode.

A bounded parser handles expressions directly; no eval, Function constructor, property access, scripts or network requests are used. Inputs are limited to 256 characters and 128 tokens. Invalid domains, overflow, division by zero, undefined tangent and unsupported syntax surface an error. Factorials require integers 0–170. Standard JavaScript floating-point arithmetic applies. This is not symbolic algebra or an arbitrary-precision engine.

The last three calculation lines exist only while the component is mounted, never change practice answers or scores, and do not pause the timer. They are not stored in backups. Existing notebook/classroom pagination remains unchanged.
